The Band

Five musicians who found each other through Copenhagen's music scene, among other places at Rytmisk Center on Vesterbro.

Our starting point is the jazz standards, which we take apart and put back together with a sway of our own — adding a little soul, a little funk and plenty of groove.

We play corporate events, wine tastings, receptions and private celebrations, and we shape the set and the volume to fit the occasion.
